## 0. TL;DR

The brief, verbatim:

> courses are sorted but still i am facing problem in using this for paper reading, do a detailed survery using chrome to find all the flows also i want to build a consistent habit of reading(but might not know the paper to read), i want to rewrite the whole process. make a markdown file for specs in plan folder. integrate it in weekly planning. so I want to be able to replan a week if required.

**What the survey found, in one sentence:** reading in FocusOS is built around papers rather than around reading. So when you have no paper ready for its next pass, the week plans zero reading.

**Seen in the app:**
1. I set aside one paper. Two others had no reading goal. The week's **Reading row then disappeared** from the ledger, and no reading was planned at all (§3, F1).
2. **Three clocks** time the same reading:
   - the pass timer, measured wall-clock from when the pass started;
   - the focus timer;
   - the reader's own "free reading" clock.

   A 3-minute session showed up twice in Today's history (F6).
3. **Stopping a paper is broken.** Finishing Read with "Set aside" left it as "Reading" at 66%, with "Start Deep dive" still offered, and it stayed at the top of "Reading now" (F3).

**The rewrite, in four ideas:**
1. **A reading habit, not a reading backlog.** You set a weekly target, like "4 sittings × 30 min". The planner always places those sittings, whether or not any paper is ready.
2. **The paper is chosen when the sitting starts, not when it's planned.** Starting a sitting opens "What should I read?":
   - your Up next queue first;
   - then papers the app already knows you want: references you marked, citations from AI notes, shelved papers whose date has come, links you dropped in the inbox;
   - finding a paper is itself a valid way to spend a sitting.
3. **One clock, and explicit exits.**
   - A pass is a series of sittings. Its minutes are the sum of the focus sessions logged against it, never wall-clock time.
   - Every sitting ends with a check-in, and every pass ends with one explicit exit: Continue, Done with this paper, Shelve until a date, or Drop.
   - Each exit maps to exactly one status that every surface agrees on.
4. **Replan the week from today.**
   - Past days count only what you actually worked.
   - Missed sittings and future sittings you haven't started go back into the pool and are placed again.
   - Manual blocks, classes, routine, commitments and anything already worked are never touched.
   - You see a preview diff, and nothing is written until you accept.

## 1. Goals and non-goals

**Goals**
- Reading happens every week at a steady rhythm, even with an empty or unready library.
- Choosing what to read takes seconds and happens at the moment of reading.
- Any sitting can end cleanly, and any paper can be stopped cleanly, at any stage.
- Reading minutes are counted once, from one source.
- The weekly planner treats reading as a habit with a target. The reading row never vanishes.
- A week can be replanned mid-week without losing history or touching anything you put there yourself.

**Non-goals (this pass)**
- External discovery feeds (arXiv RSS, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar alerts) and automatic metadata lookup from a DOI or arXiv link. These are §13 open questions. Phase 1 discovery uses only data the app already holds.
- Rewriting the reading window's UI from plan/12. We fix its first-render bug and make it the default destination; we don't redesign it.
- Changing Keshav's three-pass method itself. Skim, Read and Deep dive stay; what changes is how they're timed, entered and exited.

## 3. Findings, ranked by how much they break the habit

**F1 — Reading disappears from the week when no paper is "ready". [app]**
- `readingCandidates` makes the reading row depend on a paper that is owed a pass *and* has a goal.
- In the test week, the Reading row showed "1h asked" while "Attention…" was owed a Read. After that Read ended "Set aside", **the row was gone** and the ledger had only the course.
- There is no habit-level target anywhere (Step 3 has only course hours).
- This is the root cause of "can't build a consistent habit".

**F2 — Knowing *what* to read is entirely on you, and the app throws away the answers it already has. [code]**
- The app collects a lot of "read this next" data and acts on none of it:
  - Skim's `referencesAlreadyRead`;
  - Read's `unreadReferencesMarked`;
  - Return-later's `backgroundToRead` (turned into generic tasks, not papers);
  - `layeredNotes.citationsToRead`;
  - paper-set "not in your library" citations.
- Shelved papers are hidden for 180 days.
- There is no queue order beyond priority and staleness, and no inbox for "a link someone sent me".

**F3 — "Stop" outcomes don't stop. [app] + [code]**
- `derivePaperStatus` (`lib/papers.ts:37`) has no branch for Read → `set-aside` or `return-later`, so the paper stays `reading` forever.
- `nextReadingPass` returns null, so the planner drops it, yet "Reading now" and the morning brief keep showing it.
- `returnAfter.revisitOn` is written and never read.
- This is why the earlier question, "how to stop the paper after skim, read etc", has no good answer today.

**F4 — Read's outcome is hidden and defaults to "Grasped it". [app]**
- Under *More options* (`paper-pass-section.tsx:418`), so saving a summary silently marks the paper finished.
- The most consequential choice on the page is the least visible one.

**F5 — Pass minutes are wall-clock. [code]**
- `finishPass` records `elapsedMinutesSince(pass.startedAt)` (`paper-pass-section.tsx:63`).
- A Read started Monday and finished Wednesday logs about 2,880 minutes. That number feeds `calibratedPassEstimate`, which sizes every future reading sitting (`lib/papers.ts:61`).
- Reading in two sittings, which is exactly what the planner's chunking (K1) proposes, is impossible to time honestly.

**F6 — Three clocks, double-counted. [app]**
- **The focus timer** (from a reading block) logs a session with `paperId`/`passNo` (`focus-session-provider.tsx:452`).
- **`finishPass`** logs a second session for the same minutes (`paper-pass-section.tsx:67`).
- **The reader's free-reading clock** logs a third whenever no pass is running, including while the tab sits idle.
- `totalReadingMinutes` sums all of them (`lib/papers.ts:12`). Focus min and the day score are inflated too.

**F7 — Starting a reading block doesn't start reading. [app]**
- The block's link goes to the detail page, not the reader.
- "Start focus" doesn't start or resume the pass.
- The pass timer and the focus timer run unaware of each other.
- A block whose paper was shelved mid-sitting keeps offering "Start focus".

**F8 — The reading goal blocks planning, not just starting. [app]**
- Papers without a goal are excluded from the week (`readingCandidates` → `needsGoal`) and only appear as notes.
- The goal is one tap from a preset, so the gate costs a trip to the paper page for every paper, during planning, which is the worst time.

**F9 — The reader's first render is broken. [app]**
- **Repro:** open `/read/<id>` for a paper with a PDF. At the default Fit width, the page is drawn mirrored and upside down at thumbnail size, and the Next.js overlay shows pdf.js's "Cannot use the same canvas during multiple render() operations".
- Choosing 100% (or any other zoom) redraws it correctly.
- **Likely cause:** the fit-width scale is computed after the first render has already started on the same canvas, and the earlier `RenderTask` isn't cancelled.

**F10 — The way into the reader is buried, and passes can't be finished there. [app]**
- "Open reading window" is the last card on `/papers/[id]`, below the pass forms and Notes.
- The reader shows the running pass but can't finish it.
- Link-only papers have no reading surface at all.

**F11 — Small, but each one adds friction. [app]**
- No arXiv/DOI metadata fetch.
- One-click delete with no confirmation.
- The goal chip shows no selected state.
- "above" in the Skim blocking message is wrong (it's to the right).
- The Clarity default is recorded as data.
- Skim and Read have no pause or cancel.

**F12 — Weekly planning can't be redone mid-week. [code]**
- `plannedMinutesForObjective` (`lib/courses.ts:294`) counts every slot on a week's days, *including past days you never worked*, as "placed". The ledger's own footnote says so: "Placed means on a day this week (including days already behind you)" (`plan/week/page.tsx:1834`).
- So a missed Monday sitting still counts as placed, and the planner never offers that work again.
- Nothing releases future, unstarted sittings for re-placement. You'd have to delete them by hand on each day.
- `commitWeek` overwrites `proposedBlocks` wholesale (`:1315`), with no history.

## 4. Design principles

1. **Plan the habit, choose the paper later.** A reading sitting needs a time and a length, not a paper.
2. **There's always an answer to "what should I read?"** Even an empty library yields one: "spend this sitting finding one".
3. **One clock.** Minutes come from focus sessions only. Everything else is a view of them.
4. **Every ending is explicit and maps to one status.** No outcome may leave a paper in a state that some surfaces treat as active and others as finished.
5. **Replan never touches anything you made, anything you worked, or the locked layer.** It previews before it writes.
6. **Reuse what exists.**
   - `objectives.ts` already labels a reading slot with no reference as generic reading (`lib/objectives.ts:117`).
   - `slotCoverageMinutes` (`lib/tracking.ts:34`) already measures what a sitting actually got.
   - Plan/16's sittings, chunking, renumbering and `keyedAfterRemoval` already handle re-placement.

## 5. The new reading model

### 5.1 Concepts

| Concept | What it is | Stored as |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading habit** | A weekly target: sittings per week × minutes per sitting, plus optional preferred days and time of day. | New `UserSettings.readingHabit` (§8) |
| **Reading sitting** | A `type: "reading"` block. It may name a paper (*pinned*) or not (*open*). An open sitting picks its paper when started. | `ScheduleSlot` with `objectiveRef: { kind: "readingHabit" }`, or `{ kind: "paper", … }` when pinned |
| **Up next** | Your ordered reading queue, the first thing the picker offers. | New `Paper.queuedAt` (order key) |
| **Inbox** | Papers captured but not yet looked at: a pasted link, a harvested citation. Triaged with one tap: queue, shelve or drop. | New `PaperStatus` value `"inbox"` |
| **Shelf** | Papers deliberately paused, with a date to come back. | New `Paper.shelvedUntil` (date) + status `"shelved"` |
| **Pass** | Skim, Read or Deep dive: unchanged in content, now made of **one or more sittings**. | `PassState` plus `sessionMinutes` derived from sessions |

### 5.2 Paper lifecycle (one status, agreed everywhere)

```
            capture (link, citation, manual add)
                         │
                       inbox ──drop──▶ dropped
                         │ queue
                         ▼
   ┌────────────── to_read (in Up next) ◀─────────── shelf date arrives
   │                     │ first sitting                    ▲
   │                     ▼                                  │
   │               reading (pass n in progress) ──shelve──▶ shelved
   │                     │ end of pass: exit choice
   │                     ├─ Continue → next pass owed (still "reading")
   │                     ├─ Done     → read
   │                     ├─ Shelve   → shelved (until date)
   │                     └─ Drop     → dropped
```

**Status changes** (`PaperStatus`):
- Add `"inbox"`, `"shelved"` and `"dropped"`.
- Keep `"archived"` only as a legacy read value (§10).

**`derivePaperStatus` becomes exhaustive.** Every pass outcome maps to exactly one status:

| Pass | Outcome (legacy value) | New status | Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skim | Keep reading (`continue`) | reading | Read owed |
| Skim | Save for later (`park`) | **shelved**, date required (default +30d) | resurfaces in Up next on the date |
| Skim | Not relevant (`drop`) | **dropped** | — |
| Skim | **Skim was enough** (new: `done`) | **read** | — |
| Read | Grasped it (`grasped`) | read | Deep dive offered, not owed |
| Read | Set aside (`set-aside`) | **shelved** | resurfaces on date |
| Read | Return later (`return-later`) | **shelved until `revisitOn`**; background items go to the **inbox as papers**, not tasks | resurfaces |
| Read | Persevere (`persevere`) | reading | Deep dive owed |
| Deep dive | finished | read | recall item in Revise (unchanged) |
| any | **Drop from any screen** | dropped | — |

**Surfaces that must read status only through `derivePaperStatus`:**
- the week planner;
- Reading now;
- the morning brief (`lib/dailyloop.ts:58` currently filters on raw `"reading"`);
- the papers list;
- the Now card's guard for a shelved paper (F7/L2).

Deep dive is offered only after Grasped it or Persevere, never after Set aside.

### 5.3 "What should I read?" — the picker

The picker opens when an *open* sitting starts, or from "Read something" on Today or `/papers`. It shows at most 5 rows in sections, each with a one-tap Start:

1. **Continue:** a pass already in progress, sorted by last session. Resuming beats starting.
2. **Up next:** the queue, in your order.
3. **Back from the shelf:** shelved papers whose `shelvedUntil ≤ today`, each with "Read now", "Shelve again" or "Drop".
4. **Suggested from your reading**, harvested from data the app already holds and de-duplicated against the library with the existing loose title match (`lib/papergroups.ts:15`):
   - Read's `unreadReferencesMarked`, and Skim's references you *haven't* read;
   - `layeredNotes.citationsToRead`;
   - paper-set citations "not in your library", ranked by how many of your papers cite them (`computeSharedCitations`);
   - Return-later background items.

   Each suggestion is a title only. Tapping it creates an inbox paper and starts a **Find-and-skim** sitting (below).
5. **Inbox:** untriaged captures.

**When every section is empty, the sitting becomes "Find a paper".** It is a first-class sitting kind, not an error. It shows a short checklist:
1. Open a source you trust (your saved sources from Settings, §13 Q3).
2. Add 1–3 candidates to the inbox.
3. Skim one.

Its minutes count toward the habit target. This handles "I might not know the paper to read": the habit never waits on the library.

**The goal gate moves to the start of a sitting (F8).**
- Picking a paper with no goal shows the six preset chips inline in the picker, and one tap starts the sitting.
- Goals are no longer required to *plan*, so `needsGoal` stops excluding papers from the week.

### 5.4 One sitting, start to finish

1. **Start.** From the Now card, a reading block in `/plan/day`, or the picker:
   - starts the focus timer, armed to the sitting's remaining minutes (plan/16 §5.5, unchanged);
   - starts or resumes the paper's next pass (`pass.status = in_progress`, `startedAt` set only if unset);
   - **opens the reader** if the paper has a PDF, or the paper's link in a new tab plus the pass panel if it doesn't (F7, F10).
2. **During.**
   - The reader's toolbar shows the one timer: the focus session, with pause and finish.
   - The pass form (takeaway, verdict or summary) is a side panel *inside the reader*, so finishing a pass never needs the detail page.
3. **End of sitting check-in.** Shown when the timer ends or Finish is pressed, in one compact sheet:
   - **Where did you get to?** Optional page number, prefilled from the reader's current page and stored on the pass.
   - **Is this pass done?**
     - *Not yet* → the pass stays in progress; the next sitting resumes it.
     - *Yes* → shows the pass's exit choices from §5.2 as **four visible buttons**, never a hidden select (F4).
   - The existing productivity rating stays in the same sheet, so there's one sheet, not two.
4. **Cancel.**
   - A pass started by mistake can be cancelled; its sessions stay logged as free reading.
   - A sitting abandoned within 2 minutes logs nothing (the existing 5-minute task-credit floor is separate and unchanged).

### 5.5 One clock

- **Source of truth:** `PomodoroSession` rows with `paperId`, plus a new `passNo`, which focus sessions already carry.
- **Pass minutes** = the sum of the sessions for that `(paperId, passNo)`. `PassState.minutes` stays as a cached copy written on pass exit, for the calibration code and older readers. `calibratedPassEstimate` then gets real per-pass minutes (F5).
- **`finishPass` stops calling `savePomodoro`.** The focus session is the record (fixes F6).
- **`PassTimer`'s wall-clock display goes away.** The reader and the pass panel show the focus timer instead.
- **The reader's free-reading clock goes away.** Reading without a sitting goes through "Read something", which starts a focus session. Idle-open tabs stop logging time.
- **Migration:** old pass and free-reading sessions stay where they are. Only new sessions follow the rule, so `totalReadingMinutes` keeps working without any rewrite.

### 5.6 Capture

- **Add by link.** `/papers`' add form accepts an arXiv/DOI URL and creates an **inbox** paper. Automatic metadata lookup is a §13 open question. Phase 1 stores the link, and the title falls back to the URL until it's edited.
- **One-tap capture of harvested references.** Every place a reference string appears gets an "Add to inbox" action:
  - the Read summary;
  - the Skim summary;
  - layered notes "Citations to read";
  - paper-set shared citations.
- **Inbox triage.** A row on `/papers` with three buttons: Queue, Shelve…, Drop. Bulk select is supported.

### 5.7 Reading habit visible on Today

- A "This week's reading" line on Today, e.g. **2 of 4 sittings · 55 of 120 min**, plus a **weeks-in-a-row** count of weeks that hit the target. This is computed, never stored, the same rule as `computeLoadIndexStreak`.
- Reading now becomes **Continue / Up next** with a Start button per row (fixes M).

## 6. Weekly planning integration

### 6.1 Step 3 — a Reading habit row

- **Added beside the course hours:** sittings per week, minutes per sitting, and an optional *preferred* time of day (morning / afternoon / evening) and days.
- **Defaults** come from `UserSettings.readingHabit`. Editing them in Step 3 changes **this week only**: it's written to `WeekPlan.readingTarget` at commit, the same snapshot rule as course `targets` (plan/14).
- **"0 this week"** is a deliberate choice: it's shown as such in Look back, not as missing data.

### 6.2 Ledger — Reading is always a row when a target is set

```
Reading habit      Asked 2h   Placed 30m   Dismissed —   To place 1h 30m
  4 × 30m · 1 pinned (Pass 2 · Attention…), 3 open
```

**Asked** = sittings × minutes. Pass-specific demand no longer *creates* the row (fixes F1). It shapes how the row is split:

- **Owed passes are pinned first.** For each paper owed a pass (`nextReadingPass`, in the §5.2 order), pin up to the sittings that pass needs, by `nextPassEstimateMinutes ÷ minutes per sitting`. The rest stay **open**.
- **Owed passes beyond the target** don't inflate Asked. They're listed under the row as "also owed: …", each with "+1 sitting" to raise this week's target.
- **Family keys:**
  - open sittings use family `readingHabit`;
  - pinned sittings keep `paper:<id>`;
  - `dismissedMinutes` works per family, as today.

### 6.3 Step 4 — placing reading sittings

- **Placement preference.** Sittings go through `placeProposals` like any other candidate, with:
  - preferred days and time of day as a soft placement preference, the same mechanism as `weeklyTargetDay` lead-time candidates;
  - **a spread rule: at most one reading sitting per day** until every working day has one. A habit is built by frequency, not by one big block.
- **Labels:**
  - open sitting: "Reading · 2 of 4" with the chip "Open — pick at start";
  - pinned sitting: "Read: Attention… · Pass 2 · 1 of 2".

## 7. Replanning a week

### 7.1 What "replan" means

Throw away the *unstarted future* of this week's plan and lay the remaining work out again across the days that are left, honestly accounting for what has and hasn't been done.

**Entry points:**
- A **"Replan from today"** button in the `/plan/week` header (enabled on any day of the current week after its first commit).
- An inline nudge in Step 4 when **any past sitting this week got under 60% of its planned minutes** (the plan/16 completion rule, `lib/tracking.ts`).
- An inline nudge after **toggling a day off** that held accepted sittings.

### 7.2 Accounting change (fixes F12)

`plannedMinutesForObjective` gains a cut-off. For dates **before today** it counts only the minutes actually worked against each slot, `min(slotCoverageMinutes(slot, sessions), plannedMinutes)`, not the planned minutes. For today and later, it counts planned minutes as now.

Effects:
- A missed Monday sitting stops counting as "placed", so its minutes become **To place** again.
- A partly worked sitting contributes what it got.
- This applies always, not only in Replan mode. The ledger footnote becomes: "Placed means worked, on days behind you, or planned, from today on".

For today itself, a sitting whose end time has passed is treated as a past day. A sitting that's under way, or later today, is treated as future.

### 7.3 What Replan releases, and what it never touches

**Released** (removed from their days and their minutes returned to the pool) when **all** of these hold:
- the slot is on today (with its start still ahead) or a later day this week;
- it is planner-made: it has `objectiveRef`, `chunk`, a planner bucket (`courseId` + `bucket`), or `assignedTaskIds` from a Step-4 task candidate;
- it is not `locked` and not of type `class`, `external` or `custom` commitment (the `commitment-` prefix);
- it has **zero** logged coverage;
- its `status` is `upcoming`.

**Never touched:**
- blocks you added by hand (no planner markers);
- classes, routine, commitments, external calendar events and the weekly-planning anchor;
- anything with logged minutes;
- anything on a past day;
- the sitting under way right now.

**Optional "keep my moves" switch, on by default:**
- Released sittings whose time you set by hand (a drag or typed time, recorded as `placedByUser: true` on accept) are *re-offered at the same time* when that time is still free.
- Only sittings the search placed are moved freely.

### 7.4 Preview, then accept

Replan is a **mode of Step 4, not a separate page**:
1. Compute the released set and remove it **in memory only**.
2. Run the ordinary proposal pipeline over today through the end of the week, with the corrected accounting (§7.2).
3. Show the grid as a diff: **kept** (unchanged), **moved** (old to new time, with a ghost at the old spot), **new**, and **dropped** (no room left, listed with its minutes under "Didn't fit").
4. **Accept replan** writes the changes day by day through the existing `acceptProposals` path:
   - a per-day `validateSlots` check;
   - sibling renumbering;
   - `keyedAfterRemoval`.

   Leaving the page writes nothing.

**Renumbering:** chunk `groupId`s are per week (plan/16), so re-placed sittings renumber together with any siblings already worked. "3 of 4" stays truthful.

### 7.5 Dismissals

- `dismissedMinutes` is **kept** across a replan by default. A dismissal was a decision about the week's total, not about one placement.
- A "Reset dismissals" checkbox in the replan bar clears them for this replan.

### 7.6 History

- New `WeekPlan.revisions?: { at, reason: "manual" | "missed" | "dayOff", released: number, placed: number, keptMoves: number }[]`, appended on each accepted replan.
- `proposedBlocks` is still overwritten wholesale. It always describes the current plan, and `revisions` explains how it got there.
- Look back shows "replanned ×2" for the prior week.

### 7.7 Edge cases

| Case | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| A sitting is running when Replan opens | It's untouched; the ledger treats it as placed. |
| A task's due date has passed | Its candidates follow existing rules; Replan doesn't revive overdue tasks. |
| A course target changed mid-week | Replan uses the live Step-3 numbers. Commit after replan snapshots them, as now. |
| A day off is set after accepting | Sittings there are released (they're unstarted). Classes and commitments on that day follow plan/15's day-off rules, unchanged. |
| A pinned reading sitting's paper was shelved or dropped | Released, and its minutes return to `readingHabit` as **open** (fixes L2 for future days). |
| Replan on Sunday evening | Shows "Nothing left to replan this week — plan next week" with a link. |

## 10. Migration and legacy data

- **Legacy `archived` papers.** Read as `dropped` if the Skim verdict was `drop`, and as `shelved` with `shelvedUntil = completedAt + 180d` if it was `park`, matching the existing Revise item's date. This is derived at read time, so no backfill is needed. An optional one-off script (`scripts/migrate-reading-status.mjs`) can write the new values.
- **Read `set-aside` / `return-later` papers.** Derive `shelved`, using `revisitOn` when present, otherwise `completedAt + 30d`.
- **Existing `PassState.minutes`** stays as recorded. Calibration ignores samples over 6 h as wall-clock artifacts (F5).
- **Old double-logged sessions** stay. History isn't rewritten.
- **Existing paper Revise items** (park) keep working. Completing one when its paper is already back in Up next is harmless.
- **Accepted `{kind:"paper"}` slots** keep counting toward `paper:<id>` exactly as now.

## 13. Open questions (each with a recommendation)

1. **Habit unit: sittings × minutes, or minutes per week?** *Recommend sittings × minutes.* A habit forms by frequency, and "4 of 4" is easier to keep than "2h". Minutes are still shown.
2. **Where does the reading goal live?** *Recommend asking at sitting start* (§5.3). The gate stays, because it improves reading, but it stops blocking planning. Alternative: drop the requirement for Skim entirely and ask after Skim, when you know enough to answer.
3. **External discovery feeds** (arXiv RSS per category, Semantic Scholar "papers citing X", saved Google Scholar alerts). *Recommend Phase 4b*, after the inbox exists. Phase 4 ships saved source *links* only, opened by the "Find a paper" sitting.
4. **arXiv/DOI metadata lookup on add.** *Recommend a server route* that calls the arXiv/Crossref public APIs (no key needed) to fill title, authors and year. It's small and removes the most common add friction, but it's a new external dependency, so it needs your approval.
5. **Default shelf time.** *Recommend 30 days*, editable in the exit sheet, instead of today's fixed 180-day park.
6. **Should Replan run automatically?** *Recommend no.* Nudge only (§7.1). A plan that rearranges itself is hard to trust.
7. **"Keep my moves" default.** *Recommend on.* You placed those deliberately.
8. **Does a "Find a paper" sitting count toward the habit streak?** *Recommend yes*, capped at one per week, so the streak rewards showing up without letting searching replace reading.
